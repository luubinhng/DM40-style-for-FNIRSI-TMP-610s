# Release Notes

## V1.9.9 DM40 Mod Package

- Distributes the supplied `APP_TMP-610S_V1.9.9_260716_dm40.bin` without changing its bytes.
- Adds English and Vietnamese introductions and installation instructions based on the bundled manual.
- Groups firmware and checksums in `binary/`, manuals and guides in `docs/`, and visual references in `images/`.
- Preserves original development manifests and documents their mismatch with the distributed binary.

The version is taken from the supplied filename. No build date, device-test result, new functionality, or measurement-accuracy improvement is inferred from that name. UI examples show development references rather than a verified changelog against stock firmware.
